Got questions?

Is it free?

Yep! Downloading and playing is free. The first 15 stages cost nothing, and one purchase unlocks all 50. Cosmetic theme packs are optional. No ads, no subscriptions, no gacha.

Do I need internet?

No. The stages and two players on one phone work offline. Online matches and sharing maps need a connection.

How do we play together?

Two players share one phone on a wide board built for two. Online, create a room and share its five-letter room code. Up to 6 can play.

Do I need an account?

Nope! Jump right in. Signing in (Settings → Account) is optional. It saves your progress, so you can pick up on another device.

Controllers and languages?

Yes to controllers! The game comes in 12 languages: English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian and Arabic.

Can I play battles and the map maker right away?

You start with the solo stages. Battle mode, online rooms and the map maker unlock one by one as you clear the free stages. The first two challenge letters are free; the rest come with the full version.
